About Kazigaon

Kazigaon is a beautiful and rapidly developing region located in the Kokrajhar District of Assam under the Bodoland Territorial Region (BTR). It is famous for the stunning Kazigaon Waterfall at Dholongjhora, peaceful natural beauty, rich tribal culture, and friendly people. Communities such as Rabha, Bodo, Garo, Bengali and Banai live together in harmony. Every year the people proudly celebrate Independence Day (15 August) and Republic Day (26 January) with great enthusiasm.

🌊 Kazigaon Waterfall (Dholongjhora)

Kazigaon Waterfall is one of the most beautiful natural attractions of Parbatjhora. Surrounded by green forests and hills, it is an ideal place for nature lovers, photographers and picnic visitors. The waterfall becomes even more attractive during the monsoon season.

🎓 Education

Kazigaon MV School

Established in 1950. Classes VI to VIII. Government school serving the village for decades.

Kazigaon High School

Established in 1967. Classes IX to X. Assamese medium government school helping students achieve quality education.

GKB Academy

A well-known educational institution in Kazigaon providing quality education to students.

ITI Kazigaon

Industrial Training Institute inaugurated in 2023. It provides vocational and skill-based education for the youth of the region.

⚽ Sports & Stadium

SNB Stadium, Kazigaon

SNB Stadium is one of the biggest sports venues in the Parbatjhora region. It hosts football tournaments including the Parbatjhora Championship Football League and encourages young sports talent.

🏥 Health Services

Kazigaon Primary Health Centre (PHC)

The PHC provides basic healthcare, immunization, outpatient services and rural medical support. Government development projects are expanding healthcare facilities in the area.

👮 Police Station

Kazigaon Police Station

Located on Alomganj Tipkai Road, Kazigaon Police Station maintains law and order and serves the entire Parbatjhora region.

🚀 Future Development

Kazigaon is developing rapidly with new roads, educational institutions, healthcare facilities and tourism projects. More development is expected in the coming years, making Kazigaon one of the emerging regions of Bodoland Territorial Region (BTR), Assam.
